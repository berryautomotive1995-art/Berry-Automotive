When to call

Signs your vehicle needs attention.

Squealing or grinding

Brake noise can point to worn parts or another issue that should be inspected.

Vibration while braking

A shake or pulse through the pedal or steering wheel deserves a closer look.

Pedal feels different

A soft, low, hard, or changing brake pedal can signal a safety concern.

Longer stopping or pulling

If the vehicle takes longer to stop or pulls while braking, schedule an inspection.

Straightforward service

What to expect from brake service

We start with the symptoms you have noticed, inspect the braking system, and identify the next practical step. Depending on the vehicle and condition, service may involve worn brake components or another issue affecting stopping performance.

Some brake work may be suitable for mobile service within 15 miles of our Durham shop. We confirm the vehicle, location, access, and repair before scheduling mobile work. If the vehicle does not feel safe to stop, do not drive it—call or text us first.
