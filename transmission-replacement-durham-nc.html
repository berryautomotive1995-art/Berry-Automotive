When to call

Signs your vehicle needs attention.

Slipping or delayed movement

Engine speed rising without matching vehicle movement can point to a transmission concern.

Harsh or unusual shifting

Sudden, delayed, or inconsistent shifts should be diagnosed.

Fluid leak

A transmission-fluid leak can lead to poor operation or damage if it is ignored.

Vehicle will not move normally

If the vehicle will not engage or move safely, call before attempting to drive it.
