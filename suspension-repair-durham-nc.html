When to call

Signs your vehicle needs attention.

Clunks or knocks

Noise over bumps or during turns can be a sign of worn suspension components.

Bouncing or rough ride

Excess movement after a bump can make the vehicle harder to control.

Uneven tire wear

Suspension wear can contribute to tires wearing unevenly or faster than expected.

Pulling or loose handling

A vehicle that wanders, pulls, or feels unstable should be inspected.
