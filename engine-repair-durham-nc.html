When to call

Signs your vehicle needs attention.

Overheating

Repeated overheating can cause serious damage. Stop driving if the temperature climbs into an unsafe range.

Knocking or unusual noise

New mechanical noises should be inspected before they become a larger failure.

Smoke, leaks, or fluid loss

Oil or coolant loss and exhaust smoke can help point toward the source of a problem.

Low power or rough running

Misfires, stalling, shaking, and loss of power can have several possible causes.

Straightforward service
